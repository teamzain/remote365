## Notes to the reviewer (paste into the Notes field)

```
Remote365 is a business tool for IT teams. A technician signs in, sees the computers their organization manages, connects to one to view and control its screen from an iPhone or iPad, and can hold a video meeting or chat with colleagues from the same app.

SIGN IN
Every feature requires an account. Please use the demo account entered above. It belongs to an isolated organization created for App Review and contains no customer data. Two-factor authentication is disabled on it.

WHAT TO TRY
1. Devices tab — the organization's computers. "Review PC" is kept online for you: tap it, choose Connect, and the remote screen appears. Drag to move the pointer, tap to click, pinch to zoom, and use the toolbar for the keyboard. Tap the red button to end the session.
2. Connect tab — "Search and connect" finds a device by name or ID; "Recent connections" reopens previous sessions.
3. Meetings tab — "New meeting" starts a video call. The camera and microphone prompts appear here and only here. Inside a meeting, the share button opens the iOS broadcast picker to share this device's screen.
4. Chat tab — message a contact and attach a photo (this triggers the photo library prompt).
5. Settings → Account → Delete Account — permanently deletes the signed-in account and all its data. Please test this on an account you create with Sign up rather than on the shared demo account.

PERMISSIONS
Camera and microphone: video meetings only. Photos: attaching an image in chat. Face ID: optional app unlock, enabled from Settings → Biometric access. Notifications: new messages and session or meeting invitations.

LOGIN SERVICES
Email and password, Google, Microsoft and Sign in with Apple (shown first on iPhone and iPad).

PURCHASES
Settings > Upgrade Plan sells the Solo, Pro and Business plans as auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly or yearly) through In-App Purchase. Prices come from the App Store. The demo account is on a trial, so any plan can be bought with a sandbox Apple Account. Restore Purchases, Manage Subscription and the Terms of Use (EULA) link are at the bottom of that screen, under the renewal terms. Purchases are verified on our server from the StoreKit 2 signed transaction before the plan changes.

The host computer must be running the Remote365 desktop app to accept connections, which is why remote control cannot be demonstrated on the reviewer's own Mac. If the host appears offline, please contact us at the phone number above and we will bring it back within minutes.
```
